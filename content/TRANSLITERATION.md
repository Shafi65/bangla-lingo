# Transliteration guide (learner-friendly)

Goal: an English speaker can read `tr` aloud and come close. This is **not** ISO 15919. It follows how Bengali is *pronounced* in Standard Colloquial Bengali, so the inherent vowel is written `o`, and silent final vowels are dropped.

## Vowels (স্বরবর্ণ)
| Letter | Sign (kar) | tr | Sounds like |
|---|---|---|---|
| অ | (inherent) | o | "o" in *or* (short) — sometimes silent at word end |
| আ | া | a | "a" in *father* |
| ই / ঈ | ি / ী | i | "ee" in *see* (no length difference in speech) |
| উ / ঊ | ু / ূ | u | "oo" in *food* |
| ঋ | ৃ | ri | "ri" in *rip* |
| এ | ে | e | "e" in *bed*, "ay" in *say*, or "a" in *cat* (এক, এখন) — audio tells them apart |
| ঐ | ৈ | oi | "oy" in *boy* |
| ও | ো | o | "o" in *go* |
| ঔ | ৌ | ou | "ow" in *low* |

## Consonants (ব্যঞ্জনবর্ণ)
Aspirated consonants add `h`. Retroflex consonants are written the same as dental ones (learners pick up the difference by ear; audio carries it).

| Letter | tr | Letter | tr | Letter | tr |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ক | k | খ | kh | গ | g |
| ঘ | gh | ঙ | ng | চ | ch |

## Conventions
- Lowercase only; no diacritics.
- Inherent vowel: write `o` where it is pronounced (বল = `bolo`), drop it where silent (আমার = `amar`).
- স is written `sh` when pronounced so (most words: সে = `she`), `s` before dental consonants (স্টেশন = `steshon`).
- Separate words with spaces as in the Bengali script.
- Accept close variants in `type_translation.accepted` (e.g. `apni`, `aapni`).
